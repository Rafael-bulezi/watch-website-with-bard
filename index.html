03 / Notes

Notes from
the cabinet.

Opinion, not advice. A few thoughts collected between viewings — about wearing one watch, reading a dial, and knowing when to stop.

Note 01 · The daily

The case for one watch

A collection is not a requirement. One watch worn daily, serviced when it asks, becomes more legible than a drawer of seldom-seen things.

Note 02 · The details

What the pushers know

A chronograph is not jewellery. Start, stop, reset — feel the action before you fall for the dial. A pusher should answer, not resist.

Note 03 · The finish

Steel, then gold

Two-tone is a bridge between a suit and a Saturday. It works when the dial stays quiet, and when the gold is the only thing asking for attention.
